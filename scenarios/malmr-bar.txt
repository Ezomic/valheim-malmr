# Malmr: with copper open to you, the vein switch decides where a pickaxe blow goes. Off, the blow
# knocks rock off the chunk it struck, as it always has. On, it goes into the deposit's bar and
# every chunk stays whole. Enough blows into the bar take the whole deposit down at once.
#
# Three blocks, each on its own deposit that `put` stands 4.5 metres in front of you. Each `put`
# takes the one before it down, so no two deposits ever stand at once.
#
# WHY 4.5 METRES. `put` measures to the deposit's pivot, and a rock4 deposit is broad: 3 metres, what
# this file used first, stood it around you. In the first run, on 2026-09-28, every copper deposit
# put 3 metres out at the height of your feet took no blow at all - three of three, in the Meadows,
# the Black Forest and the Mistlands - and nor did one put 2.7 metres out below you on a slope. Each
# time Devkit put your eye inside the box of its chunks. The only one that took blows from 3 metres
# (malmr-wear.txt) had you 2.5 metres above its foot on a steeper slope, eye above its box, swinging
# down onto a top that must be wide to be within reach there. 4.5 is inferred from that run, not
# measured. If the swings still miss, read distance= on the `malmr progress` line after the `put`:
# the metres from your feet to its nearest chunk, 0.0 when you are standing in it. Since Devkit
# 1b48353 the `put` note also says outright when you are standing inside what it made, and a
# missed `swing` gives the distance from the blow's origin to the nearest face, not from your eye
# to the box round every chunk.
#
# That run also stood every deposit after the first ON the one before it, 3 metres up: Devkit's
# `put` found no earlier copy to take down, because it looked for them by GameObject name, and a
# live MineRock5 renames its GameObject in Awake. Devkit 1b48353 fixed that: it finds them by the
# ZDO's prefab and takes them down before it measures the ground. A Devkit older than that still
# stands blocks 2 and 3 on a deposit floating on the last one, and they fail for that.
#
#   1. By hand. Vein mining off, Pickaxes 30, the Elder killed twice through Vandi. One swing
#      leaves at least one chunk below full health, and the bar stays at damage=0.0.
#   2. Into the bar. The same fixture with vein mining on. The bar is empty before the first
#      swing and off zero after it, and after each of two swings no chunk is hurt.
#   3. The whole deposit. Pickaxes 100, which makes every blow as hard as it gets, and up to fifty
#      swings. The first is checked on its own to have gone into the bar. After the rest the
#      deposit is gone, and copper ore came out of it: at least one, on the ground and in the pack
#      counted together (see WHAT IT CANNOT SEE for why together).
#
# Blocks 1 and 2 stand exactly on copper's line, Pickaxes 30 with two kills, because that is the
# least a player needs and the place an off-by-one would show.
#
# Every swing is a real one: Devkit's `swing` calls Humanoid.StartAttack as the attack key does. A
# swing only counts once it has LANDED, which means the deposit's ZDO was written. In vein mode the
# only thing Malmr writes there is the bar, so a swing that lands while `hurt` reads no chunk below
# full health is a blow the bar took. That is how block 2 sees its second swing raise the bar
# without a number to compare it to: the swing landed, and no chunk moved.
#
# NEEDS VANDI. Copper's boss half is seeded through Vandi's own kill record,
# vandi_p_<playerid>_defeated_gdking at 2 (the one-star kill, the layout in Vandi's Kills.cs), and
# the key is removed at the end. A character with real Vandi kills of the Elder in this world loses
# them. Without Vandi `printed bosscount=vandi` fails first, and copper reads shut, because the
# game's own tally would want a real kill.
#
# SINGLEPLAYER, in a TEST WORLD, with the default config (Copper at 30 on defeated_gdking). Face open,
# flat ground, since the 4.5 metres assume the deposit's foot is level with yours. `biome Meadows`
# takes you to the Meadows if you are not there, and leaves you where you stand if you are. Anywhere
# but the Mistlands would do, since only there is copper left to the hand.
#
# FEED THE CHARACTER FIRST with Devkit's food button. God mode does not stop stamina use, and block 3
# is fifty swings in one step. A hungry character can run dry and fail it on stamina, which the
# step's note names.
#
# It LEAVES BEHIND god and ghost mode on (Devkit cannot switch them off again) and the ore block 3
# dropped. No deposit is left: the third breaks, and the `put`s took the first two down. Pickaxes is
# put back, the Vandi key is removed and vein mining ends off. Devkit empties the pack when a run
# ends and hands back its kit, so the pickaxe and any ore you picked up go with it.
#
# What a broken build reads, block by block:
#   - 1: a build that sends blows to the bar whatever the switch says reads `hurt` 0, and damage
#     above 0.0.
#   - 2: a build that lets the blow through to the chunk reads `hurt` 1 or more. A build whose owner
#     never counts the blow writes nothing, so `swing` fails with "none of them changed".
#   - every block: a build that reads a deposit's name off its GameObject answers
#     "malmr progress=none" to every `malmr progress rock4_copper_frac`, standing right at one,
#     because a live MineRock5 renames its GameObject in Awake. That was the first run - see
#     Deposits.PrefabName.
#   - 3: a full bar that never breaks the deposit, or breaks only part of it, leaves it standing,
#     so `malmr progress` prints its line and progress=none fails. The note carries its percent and
#     how many chunks are left. A build that mined it out by hand instead is caught earlier, by the
#     `hurt 0` after the first swing, since fifty hand swings at Pickaxes 100 could also empty it.
#
# WHAT IT CANNOT SEE:
#   - How MUCH ore came out. The drop table rolls, so the check is at least one, not a number. And
#     it is asked of the ground and the pack together with Devkit's `found`, because ore that lands
#     within two metres of you flies into your pack: `dropped` alone would fail the day every piece
#     landed in reach, `carrying` alone the day none did. The ground and the pack are cleared of
#     copper ore before block 3, so whatever `found` counts came out of that deposit.
#   - The bar and the gold Vein marker. They are IMGUI and no step reads them, so they are checked
#     by eye against mockup A.
#   - A pickaxe too weak for the rock. The bar then adds nothing and writes nothing, which `swing`
#     cannot tell from a miss, so it fails and names the tool tiers. The black metal pickaxe is well
#     above copper's for that reason.
#   - Two players on one deposit. That needs two clients.
#
# If block 3 runs out of swings with the deposit still standing, read the percent on the failing
# line. That would be a deposit bigger than fifty black metal swings at Pickaxes 100, not the rule
# failing, and no vanilla copper deposit is expected to be that big.
#
# The `logged` lines are a backstop, not the proof. Malmr says each of those warnings once per
# session, so one already said before this run would not be said again.

god
ghost

# ---- the fixture. Through zero first, for the reason malmr-unlock-levels.txt gives, then copper's
# own level. A black metal pickaxe, whose tool tier is well above copper's, so no blow is refused as
# too hard.

skill Pickaxes 0
skill Pickaxes 30

key vandi_p_$player_defeated_gdking 2
wait 2
keyis vandi_p_$player_defeated_gdking 2

give PickaxeBlackMetal:1
tool PickaxeBlackMetal

biome Meadows

console malmr
printed bosscount=vandi yes
printed Copper unlock=30 open=yes boss=defeated_gdking kills=2/2 missing=none yes

# ---- 1. by hand. Vein mining off, and the same swing a player makes without Malmr. At least one
# chunk comes out below full health, and nothing went into the bar.

console malmr vein off
printed malmr vein=off yes

put rock4_copper_frac 4.5
swing rock4_copper_frac 1
hurt rock4_copper_frac 1+

console malmr progress rock4_copper_frac
printed prefab=rock4_copper_frac metal=Copper open=yes yes
printed damage=0.0 yes

# ---- 2. into the bar. A fresh deposit with vein mining on, and its bar starts empty. One swing
# lands and not one chunk is below full health, and the bar is off zero. owner=self says this
# machine owns the deposit, so the blow took the on-the-spot road through the owner's handler
# rather than the network. The second swing lands too and still hurts nothing.
#
# Two swings and not more. A pickaxe strikes every chunk its arc reaches and each one is a blow into
# the bar, but two swings at Pickaxes 30 are nowhere near a copper deposit's worth.

console malmr vein on
printed malmr vein=on yes

put rock4_copper_frac 4.5
console malmr progress rock4_copper_frac
printed prefab=rock4_copper_frac metal=Copper open=yes yes
printed handonly=no yes
printed damage=0.0 yes

swing rock4_copper_frac 1
hurt rock4_copper_frac 0

console malmr progress rock4_copper_frac
printed prefab=rock4_copper_frac yes
printed damage=0.0 no
printed owner=self yes
printed stale=no yes

swing rock4_copper_frac 1
hurt rock4_copper_frac 0

# ---- 3. the whole deposit. Pickaxes 100, so the bar fills in the fewest swings. The ground and the
# pack are cleared of copper ore first. Block 1 knocked some loose, and without this `found` could count
# that instead.

skill Pickaxes 100

sweep
strip CopperOre
dropped CopperOre 0
carrying CopperOre 0

put rock4_copper_frac 4.5
console malmr progress rock4_copper_frac
printed prefab=rock4_copper_frac metal=Copper open=yes yes
printed damage=0.0 yes

# One swing on its own, and it went into the bar.

swing rock4_copper_frac 1
hurt rock4_copper_frac 0

# Up to forty-nine more. `swing` stops early and passes when the deposit is gone. Gone is then asked
# of `malmr progress`, which looks ten metres out, and you have not moved, so progress=none can only
# mean it broke.

swing rock4_copper_frac 49
wait 1

console malmr progress rock4_copper_frac
printed progress=none yes

# The ore. A deposit that broke without dropping anything reads 0 here, however green the lines
# above are - which is why this is the last word on block 3 rather than the eye check it used to be.

found CopperOre 1+ 20

logged did not break whole no
logged could not finish breaking no
logged could not be sent no
logged could not be counted no

# ---- put back what can be put back.

console malmr vein off
printed malmr vein=off yes
unkey vandi_p_$player_defeated_gdking
skill Pickaxes back
