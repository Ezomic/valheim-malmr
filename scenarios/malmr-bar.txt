# Malmr: with copper open to you, the vein switch decides where a pickaxe blow goes. Off, the blow
# knocks rock off the chunk it struck, as it always has. On, it goes into the deposit's bar and
# every chunk stays whole. Enough blows into the bar take the whole deposit down at once.
#
# Three blocks, each on its own deposit that `put` stands 4.5 metres in front of you. Each `put`
# takes the one before it down, so no two deposits ever stand at once.
#
# WHY 4.5 METRES. `put` measures to the deposit's pivot, and a rock4 deposit is broad: 3 metres, what
# this file used first, stood it around you. In the first run, on 2026-09-28, every copper deposit
# put 3 metres out at the height of your feet took no blow at all - three of three, in the Meadows,
# the Black Forest and the Mistlands - and nor did one put 2.7 metres out below you on a slope. Each
# time Devkit put your eye inside the box of its chunks. The only one that took blows from 3 metres
# (malmr-wear.txt) had you 2.5 metres above its foot on a steeper slope, eye above its box, swinging
# down onto a top that must be wide to be within reach there. 4.5 is inferred from that run, not
# measured. If the swings still miss, read the Devkit notes, not Malmr's: since Devkit 1b48353 the
# `put` note says outright when you are standing inside what it made, and a missed `swing` gives
# the distance from the blow's origin to the nearest face beside the pickaxe's reach. In the second
# and third runs the `put` note said you stood inside the deposit on nearly every `put`, here, in
# malmr-stone.txt and in malmr-mistlands.txt, and every swing at them landed anyway, so 4.5 stays.
#
# NOT distance= on the `malmr progress` line. That is from your feet to the nearest chunk's BOX
# (Deposits.Distance), and a rock4 deposit's 130 overlapping boxes reach well past its faces, so it
# reads 0.0 at 4.5 metres whether you stand in the rock or not. The second run read 0.0 with no
# "standing inside" note and the nearest face 1.3 metres from the blow; the third run's
# malmr-wear.txt read 0.0 with the nearest face 2.0 metres off, out of the pickaxe's 1.8. This file
# used to call it "0.0 when you are standing in it", which sent the third run's reading of the logs
# the wrong way.
#
# FLAT GROUND, since the third run. `flatten 0 0 10` levels ten metres round you to the ground under
# your feet before anything is put, so every deposit's foot is level with yours. The file used to
# ask for flat ground in words and take whatever the file before it left you on, and what a swing
# puts into the bar turned out to depend on that (see HOW MANY SWINGS), as does whether it reaches
# at all: the third run's malmr-wear.txt stood 3.4 metres above its deposit's foot and missed.
# `landed` first, because `flatten` refuses while you are still above the ground after a teleport.
#
# The first run also stood every deposit after the first ON the one before it, 3 metres up: Devkit's
# `put` found no earlier copy to take down, because it looked for them by GameObject name, and a
# live MineRock5 renames its GameObject in Awake. Devkit 1b48353 fixed that: it finds them by the
# ZDO's prefab and takes them down before it measures the ground. A Devkit older than that still
# stands blocks 2 and 3 on a deposit floating on the last one, and they fail for that.
#
#   1. By hand. Vein mining off, Pickaxes 30, the Elder killed twice through Vandi. One swing
#      leaves at least one chunk below full health, and the bar stays at damage=0.0.
#   2. Into the bar. The same fixture with vein mining on. The bar is empty before the first
#      swing and off zero after it, and after each of two swings no chunk is hurt.
#   3. The whole deposit. Pickaxes 100, which makes every blow as hard as it gets. One swing is
#      checked on its own to have gone into the bar, then up to 200 more in a single `swing` step
#      (see HOW MANY SWINGS). After them the deposit is gone, and copper ore came out of it: at
#      least one, on the ground and in the pack counted together (see WHAT IT CANNOT SEE for why
#      together).
#
# HOW MANY SWINGS. rock4_copper_frac is 130 chunks of 50, so 6500 in a fresh world, and Pickaxes
# 100 rolls every blow at 0.85 to 1.0 of the tool's damage (Skills.GetRandomSkillFactor). What one
# black metal swing puts into the bar has been measured three times, and it is not one number:
#   - the second run, on 2026-09-28: fifty swings, damage=3004.3, so 60.1 a swing.
#   - the third run, the same day: 1, 49 and 40 swings, 4085.3, then fifty more, 2259.9. That is
#     45.4 and 45.2 a swing, and after 140 the bar stood at 97% with the deposit whole, as it should
#     be short of full. The budget ran out, not the mod.
#   - the third run's malmr-stone.txt, the same rock4 shape: 40.0 and 40.3 a swing.
# The reason is Attack.DoMeleeAttack (read with ilspycmd). A pickaxe on a deposit counts every chunk
# collider its rays strike as one thing struck, and the ground as one more, and when there is more
# than one, each takes its damage divided by 0.75 times how many there were. The ground takes
# nothing and still counts: one chunk and the ground is 60.3 / 1.5 = 40.2, the stone run to the
# decimal. Two chunks and no ground is 80 between them, two chunks and the ground 54. So the rate is
# set by what the swing's rays happen to cross, which the ground, where you stand and how the rock
# sits all move, and the third run's `put` notes had you standing inside the deposit's outer chunks
# every time. A swing by hand divides the same way, so the bar still takes exactly what the same
# swings would have dealt: this is the game, not Malmr.
#
# So block 3 does not guess a rate. Since Devkit 686ff58 one `swing` step takes up to 300 swings,
# stops to let the stamina bar fill instead of failing when it runs dry, and passes when the deposit
# is gone part-way. Before that it took fifty, and a `swing` whose deposit is already gone fails
# before it starts, so the second and third runs split the deposit into fifty-swing steps that had to
# put the break in the last one: 140 fell short at 45 a swing, and 130 then 180 would have broken it
# a step early at the second run's 60. One step of 200 covers any rate down to 32.5 a swing, below
# the 40.2 of one chunk and the ground. Its note says how many swings the deposit took ("swung N of
# 200 ... gone after that"), and 6500 over N is the rate.
#
# The black metal pickaxe is 210 durability and loses one a swing however many chunks it strikes
# (DoMeleeAttack takes it once per swing that struck anything, after all of its hits), so the 204
# swings this file can make leave it whole. Devkit empties the pack at the end of EVERY scenario,
# not only of a Run all (Report calls TidyPack), and `give` makes a fresh pickaxe, so nothing another
# file did to one reaches this one.
#
# Blocks 1 and 2 stand exactly on copper's line, Pickaxes 30 with two kills, because that is the
# least a player needs and the place an off-by-one would show.
#
# Every swing is a real one: Devkit's `swing` calls Humanoid.StartAttack as the attack key does. A
# swing only counts once it has LANDED, which means the deposit's ZDO was written. In vein mode the
# only thing Malmr writes there is the bar, so a swing that lands while `hurt` reads no chunk below
# full health is a blow the bar took. That is how block 2 sees its second swing raise the bar
# without a number to compare it to: the swing landed, and no chunk moved.
#
# NEEDS VANDI. Copper's boss half is seeded through Vandi's own kill record,
# vandi_p_<playerid>_defeated_gdking at 2 (the one-star kill, the layout in Vandi's Kills.cs), and
# the key is removed at the end. A character with real Vandi kills of the Elder in this world loses
# them. Without Vandi `printed bosscount=vandi` fails first, and copper reads shut, because the
# game's own tally would want a real kill.
#
# SINGLEPLAYER, in a TEST WORLD, with the default config (Copper at 30 on defeated_gdking). Face open,
# somewhere `flatten` can level (not a cliff edge, not in water), since the 4.5 metres assume the
# deposit's foot is level with yours. `biome Meadows` takes you to the Meadows if you are not there,
# and leaves you where you stand if you are. Anywhere but the Mistlands would do, since only there
# is copper left to the hand.
#
# FEED THE CHARACTER FIRST with Devkit's food button. God mode does not stop stamina use, and block 3
# is up to 200 swings in one step. `swing` stops and lets the bar fill whenever it runs dry, so a
# hungry character only takes longer, and the note counts the stops. It fails the step by name when
# the bar stops coming back: encumbered, or something draining it.
#
# It LEAVES BEHIND god and ghost mode on (Devkit cannot switch them off again), the levelled ground
# and the ore block 3 dropped. No deposit is left: the third breaks, and the `put`s took the first
# two down. Pickaxes is put back, the Vandi key is removed and vein mining ends off. Devkit empties
# the pack when the scenario ends and hands back its kit, so the pickaxe and any ore you picked up
# go with it.
#
# What a broken build reads, block by block:
#   - 1: a build that sends blows to the bar whatever the switch says reads `hurt` 0, and damage
#     above 0.0.
#   - 2: a build that lets the blow through to the chunk reads `hurt` 1 or more. A build whose owner
#     never counts the blow writes nothing, so `swing` fails with "none of them changed".
#   - every block: a build that reads a deposit's name off its GameObject answers
#     "malmr progress=none" to every `malmr progress rock4_copper_frac`, standing right at one,
#     because a live MineRock5 renames its GameObject in Awake. That was the first run - see
#     Deposits.PrefabName.
#   - 3: a full bar that never breaks the deposit, or breaks only part of it, leaves it standing,
#     so `malmr progress` prints its line and progress=none fails. The note carries its percent and
#     how many chunks are left. A build that mined it out by hand instead is caught earlier, by the
#     `hurt 0` after the first swing, since 200 hand swings at Pickaxes 100 could also empty it.
#
# WHAT IT CANNOT SEE:
#   - How MUCH ore came out. The drop table rolls, so the check is at least one, not a number. And
#     it is asked of the ground and the pack together with Devkit's `found`, because ore that lands
#     within two metres of you flies into your pack: `dropped` alone would fail the day every piece
#     landed in reach, `carrying` alone the day none did. The ground and the pack are cleared of
#     copper ore before block 3, so whatever `found` counts came out of that deposit.
#   - The bar and the gold Vein marker. They are IMGUI and no step reads them, so they are checked
#     by eye against mockup A.
#   - A pickaxe too weak for the rock. The bar then adds nothing and writes nothing, which `swing`
#     cannot tell from a miss, so it fails and names the tool tiers. The black metal pickaxe is well
#     above copper's for that reason.
#   - Two players on one deposit. That needs two clients.
#   - The key itself. The run switches vein mining only from the console, because no step can
#     press a key or take the window's focus away. See THE KEY, BY HAND below.
#
# THE KEY, BY HAND, after the run or on its own. Any world, a pickaxe in hand. `malmr` in the
# console prints vein=on or vein=off, and each step starts from `malmr vein off`.
#   1. Tap Left Alt once, quickly. Expected: a moment after you let go, the gold Vein shows above
#      the crosshair and `malmr` reads vein=on. Tap it again: Vein goes, and vein=off.
#   2. Alt+Tab to another window and Alt+Tab straight back. Expected: no Vein, and vein=off. Do it
#      three times: quickly both ways; holding Alt on the switcher for a second both ways; and out
#      by Alt+Tab, back by clicking the game's window.
#   3. Hold Left Alt, press and let go of W, then let go of Alt, all inside half a second. Expected:
#      no Vein, and vein=off. Then the same with E.
#   4. Tap Left Alt twice, as fast as you can. Expected: no Vein a moment later, and vein=off. Two
#      taps are two switches, however close together; a Vein here means the second tap swallowed
#      the first.
# A Vein after step 2 is the bug of the first run, on 2026-09-28: Alt+Tab switched vein mining on
# and it stayed on for the rest of the session. See VeinMode for the rule that closes it.
#
# If block 3 runs out of swings with the deposit still standing, the `swing` note reads "swung 200 of
# 200", and the failing `malmr progress` line gives the percent and damage=, which over 201 swings is
# the rate. A bar at 100% over a standing deposit is a break that never finished. A bar short of full
# is the bar counting less than each blow dealt, since swings that reach the rock average 40 or more
# (see HOW MANY SWINGS) unless their rays also cross something else, a bush or another rock.
#
# The `logged` lines are a backstop, not the proof. Malmr says each of those warnings once per
# session, so one already said before this run would not be said again.

god
ghost

# ---- the fixture. Through zero first, for the reason malmr-unlock-levels.txt gives, then copper's
# own level. A black metal pickaxe, whose tool tier is well above copper's, so no blow is refused as
# too hard.

skill Pickaxes 0
skill Pickaxes 30

key vandi_p_$player_defeated_gdking 2
wait 2
keyis vandi_p_$player_defeated_gdking 2

give PickaxeBlackMetal:1
tool PickaxeBlackMetal

biome Meadows

# Flat ground under every deposit this file puts, for the reasons under FLAT GROUND.

landed
flatten 0 0 10

console malmr
printed bosscount=vandi yes
printed Copper unlock=30 open=yes boss=defeated_gdking kills=2/2 missing=none yes

# ---- 1. by hand. Vein mining off, and the same swing a player makes without Malmr. At least one
# chunk comes out below full health, and nothing went into the bar.

console malmr vein off
printed malmr vein=off yes

put rock4_copper_frac 4.5
swing rock4_copper_frac 1
hurt rock4_copper_frac 1+

console malmr progress rock4_copper_frac
printed prefab=rock4_copper_frac metal=Copper open=yes yes
printed damage=0.0 yes

# ---- 2. into the bar. A fresh deposit with vein mining on, and its bar starts empty. One swing
# lands and not one chunk is below full health, and the bar is off zero. owner=self says this
# machine owns the deposit, so the blow took the on-the-spot road through the owner's handler
# rather than the network. The second swing lands too and still hurts nothing.
#
# Two swings and not more. A pickaxe strikes every chunk its arc reaches and each one is a blow into
# the bar, but two swings at Pickaxes 30 are nowhere near a copper deposit's worth.

console malmr vein on
printed malmr vein=on yes

put rock4_copper_frac 4.5
console malmr progress rock4_copper_frac
printed prefab=rock4_copper_frac metal=Copper open=yes yes
printed handonly=no yes
printed damage=0.0 yes

swing rock4_copper_frac 1
hurt rock4_copper_frac 0

console malmr progress rock4_copper_frac
printed prefab=rock4_copper_frac yes
printed damage=0.0 no
printed owner=self yes
printed stale=no yes

swing rock4_copper_frac 1
hurt rock4_copper_frac 0

# ---- 3. the whole deposit. Pickaxes 100, so the bar fills in the fewest swings. The ground and the
# pack are cleared of copper ore first. Block 1 knocked some loose, and without this `found` could count
# that instead.

skill Pickaxes 100

sweep
strip CopperOre
dropped CopperOre 0
carrying CopperOre 0

put rock4_copper_frac 4.5
console malmr progress rock4_copper_frac
printed prefab=rock4_copper_frac metal=Copper open=yes yes
printed damage=0.0 yes

# One swing on its own, and it went into the bar.

swing rock4_copper_frac 1
hurt rock4_copper_frac 0

# The rest in one step, up to two hundred more, which cover any rate the game can deal here: see HOW
# MANY SWINGS in the header. `swing` rests for stamina on its own, and stops early and passes when
# the deposit is gone. Gone is then asked of `malmr progress`, which looks ten metres out, and you
# have not moved, so progress=none can only mean it broke. The five seconds before it are for a
# break still coming down a few chunks a frame when the last swing ends, malmr-stone.txt's reason.

swing rock4_copper_frac 200
wait 5

console malmr progress rock4_copper_frac
printed progress=none yes

# The ore. A deposit that broke without dropping anything reads 0 here, however green the lines
# above are - which is why this is the last word on block 3 rather than the eye check it used to be.

found CopperOre 1+ 20

logged did not break whole no
logged could not finish breaking no
logged could not be sent no
logged could not be counted no

# ---- put back what can be put back.

console malmr vein off
printed malmr vein=off yes
unkey vandi_p_$player_defeated_gdking
skill Pickaxes back
